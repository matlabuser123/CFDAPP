## Stopping rule

Stop at the first failed gate. Classify it as new regression / pre-existing
limitation / environmental / invalid gate. Preserve the failure. Fix only the
demonstrated issue. Re-run the affected gate, then re-run what depends on it.

Do not weaken a threshold. Do not silently change an exclusion.
