feat(gpu): complete resident CUDA SIMPLE pipeline

GPU-DISC-001 ports the finite-volume discretization to CUDA; GPU-PIPE-001 makes
the SIMPLE outer iteration device-resident end to end. In steady state the loop
uploads nothing, and besides the one face-flux download the convergence
definition requires, it brings back 40 bytes in 2D and 48 in 3D.

- CUDA discretization: device mesh, gradients, diffusion, convection, boundary
  conditions, momentum assembly and response coefficients, Rhie-Chow predicted
  face flux, pressure-correction assembly, velocity and face-flux correction
- persistent GPU fields: velocity, pressure, face flux and effective viscosity
  carried across outer iterations instead of re-uploaded every iteration
- the pressure system, p' and the Krylov workspace stay device-resident
- momentum is assembled and solved on the device through the same bicgstabCore
  the host entry point uses: one algorithm, three entry points, no new solver
- device-side finiteness guards replace the host full-field checks

Verification, all on WSL2 + CUDA 12.9 + RTX 5000 Ada (sm_89):

- every discretization operator bitwise CPU/GPU equal (15 differential gates)
- the resident loop bitwise against the pre-residency path: 12 cases,
  217,755 values, including two 60-iteration runs
- CPU vs GPU production solves on 15 cases plus a 160/320/640 fixed-budget
  ladder; worst discrepancy 9.6e-10 against the repository's adopted 1e-6 bound
- 12 negative controls detected, compute-sanitizer clean (memcheck, initcheck,
  synccheck, racecheck), lifecycle and 20^2-640^2 performance evidence

640^2 GPU/CPU speed-up 3.593x -> 17.362x; crossover moves from 320^2 to 160^2.
The dominant remaining cost is the BiCGSTAB reduction round trips.

The CPU backend is untouched and remains the numerical reference. The known GPU
BiCGSTAB restart asymmetry is unchanged (PressureCorrectionFailure at 1845
iterations) and stays recorded as open technical debt. The device path is
reachable only through the C++ SIMPLESettings API; the case format has no
enableGpuDiscretization key.
